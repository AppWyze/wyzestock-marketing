How it works

Figures first, words second

The AI never does the arithmetic. Wyzestock does, and the AI explains it.

1

Wyzestock works out the facts

The figures for the question, from stock and orders to the forecast and model stock, are calculated in the database first.

2

The AI puts them into words

A short explanation in plain English, written for a stock planner, with a suggested next step.

3

Every number is checked

Each figure in the answer is compared with the facts. If one does not match, the answer is rejected and the standard wording is shown instead.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Does the AI make up numbers?
No. Wyzestock calculates every figure first, and any answer containing a number that is not in the facts is thrown away.
Is my data used to train AI models?
No. Only the figures needed for the question are sent, and they are not used for training.
Can we switch it off?
Yes, per company. Everything else in Wyzestock works the same without it.
Does it cost extra?
Talk to us. AI Insights is available on request and has a daily limit on use.
