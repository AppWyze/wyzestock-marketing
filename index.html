Stock position

See what you hold against what you should hold

Every item at every location, with its model stock beside what is actually on the shelf. The gap, in units and in rand, is the money Wyzestock can free up, or the sales it can protect.

  • On hand, allocated, available and on purchase order
  • Model stock, reorder point and safety stock for each location
  • Days of cover and the date it will run out
  • Click any item for its full detail, forecast and bill of material

Exceptions

Start with the items that need you today

Wyzestock sorts the exceptions out of thousands of item and location combinations and puts a value on each, so the first thing you look at is the thing that costs the most.

  • Stock-outs and items that will run out before stock can arrive
  • Excess above model stock, valued at cost
  • Slow-moving stock by 30, 60, 90 and 120 days without a sale
  • Purchase orders that are more than you need
Replenishment

Know what to order, from whom, and how much

Suggested orders for every supplier, worked out from the model stock, what is already on order and the lead time, and rounded the way you actually buy.

  • Rounded to the minimum order quantity and the pack or pallet multiple
  • Economic order quantity alongside, for the items where it matters
  • Open purchase orders, with the overdue ones highlighted
  • Filter by supplier, location, group or category and export to Excel

Distribution

Move stock before you buy more of it

One depot is short while another holds too much. Wyzestock suggests the transfers that cover the shortage from stock you already own, from the locations you choose, in the order you choose.

  • Transfers from your production or central warehouse first, then from other depots
  • Lines that cannot be covered, so you know what to make or buy
  • The value of every transfer and every uncovered line
  • Press any line to see the item at every location

Sales month to date against forecast, the run rate for the rest of the month, stock cover and production against plan, by range and by item. Emailed as an Excel report after the morning update.

Bills of material

From finished goods to every component behind them

Wyzestock reads your recipes from the ERP, works out component demand from finished-good sales, and shows what each product is made from and where each component is used.

  • Component forecasts from finished-good demand, at the production location
  • Made from: every component, every level, with the stock behind it
  • Used in: which finished goods a short component will stop
  • Raw materials, packaging and consumables planned like everything else

  • Explain these figures, for any item at any location
  • What needs attention today, ranked, with a suggested next step
  • Monthly commentary for the demand management meeting
  • A review of the forecasts that look out of line

Automatic sync

No exports. No spreadsheets. Nobody has to remember to run anything.

A small service on your server reads stock, sales, open orders, purchase orders and recipes from your ERP twice a day. Wyzestock recalculates everything and the screens are simply current.

  • Read-only: nothing in your ERP is changed
  • Twice a day, at 07:00 and 14:00, automatically
  • If a load fails or is late, we know about it before you do
